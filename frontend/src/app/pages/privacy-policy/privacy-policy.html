Your privacy matters to us at Shiv Agri Consultancy & Laboratory

Shiv Agri Consultancy & Laboratory ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of the farmers, clients, and visitors who use our website and laboratory services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal details you provide directly — such as your name, phone number, email address, and postal address — when you register, complete your profile, request a soil, water, or fertilizer test, or contact us.
  • Farm and sample data — including farm location, crop details, and the samples you submit for laboratory testing, along with the resulting reports.
  • Account information — login credentials and role information used to secure access to your account.
  • Usage information — basic technical data such as browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected automatically to help us improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and deliver soil, water, and fertilizer testing services and share your reports.
  • Create and manage your account and farm records.
  • Provide agricultural consultancy, recommendations, and support.
  • Respond to your enquiries and communicate important updates about your requests.
  • Maintain the security of our systems and comply with legal obligations.
  • Improve our website, laboratory processes, and overall service quality.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it only in limited circumstances: with trusted service providers who help us operate our platform (such as hosting and messaging providers), when required by law or a valid legal request, or with your consent. Any third parties who handle your data on our behalf are expected to protect it and use it only for the purposes we specify.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information and test reports for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services, and thereafter only as required to meet legal, accounting, or reporting obligations.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. You can also update most of your details directly from your account. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

7. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect some features.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for farmers and businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
